RESIDENTIAL RENTAL AGREEMENT

SECTION 1: PROPERTY AND TERM
This agreement covers the residential property located at House 45,
Block F, DHA Phase 5, Lahore. The lease term is 11 months commencing
from the date of signing, renewable by mutual written consent.

SECTION 2: RENT AND SECURITY DEPOSIT
Monthly rent is set at PKR 85,000, payable in advance on or before the
5th of each month. A security deposit equal to 2 months rent, totaling
PKR 170,000, is required at the time of signing and shall be refunded
within 15 days of vacating the property, less any deductions for damages.

SECTION 3: MAINTENANCE RESPONSIBILITIES
The Landlord is responsible for major structural repairs, plumbing
issues not caused by tenant negligence, and electrical wiring problems.
The Tenant is responsible for minor repairs, cleanliness, and any damage
caused by tenant or guests. The Tenant must report major issues to the
Landlord within 48 hours of discovery.

SECTION 4: TERMINATION AND NOTICE
Either party may terminate this agreement by providing 60 days written
notice prior to the intended termination date. Early termination by the
Tenant before completion of 11 months will result in forfeiture of the
security deposit unless mutually agreed otherwise in writing.

SECTION 5: UTILITIES
The Tenant shall be responsible for payment of electricity, gas, internet,
and water bills. The Landlord shall be responsible for property tax and
building maintenance charges if applicable.

SECTION 6: RESTRICTIONS
Subletting the property without written consent of the Landlord is strictly
prohibited. The Tenant shall not use the premises for any illegal purpose
or commercial activity without prior written approval.

SECTION 7: RENEWAL TERMS
Upon completion of the initial 11 month term, the agreement may be renewed
for an additional term with rent subject to a maximum increase of 10
percent, to be mutually agreed upon 30 days before the term expires.
